Verdict

Approve. The change is small, correctly ordered (new patterns precede the generic -virt/-cnv fallbacks and win first-match), and consistent with existing view/pattern conventions. Main gaps are missing dedicated test coverage and a couple of open questions about scope/readiness that don't block merge but should be confirmed with the author.

What this PR does

  • Adds two substring patterns (-acm-cnv-, -acm-virt-) to layeredProductPatterns in pkg/variantregistry/ocp.go, placed before the generic -virt/-cnv entries so ACM+Virt interop jobs get their own lp-interop--acm-virt classification.
  • Adds a new Component Readiness dashboard view, 4.22-LP-Interop--lpMainline, filtered by Owner: mpiit and Network: ovn with an any-value LayeredProduct filter.
  • Regenerates pkg/variantregistry/snapshot.yaml, reclassifying one existing job from virt to lp-interop--acm-virt.
