What this PR does

  • Adds seedReleasePayloads() to cmd/sippy/seed_data.go: 6 release_tags across 4.21/4.22 nightly/ci streams, with release_job_runs, release_pull_requests, release_repositories, and correct previous-accepted-tag linkage per release/stream.
  • Adds seedBugsAndTriages(): 4 bugs with Jira-style URLs/statuses linked to seeded tests/jobs, plus 2 triage records linked to regressions.
  • Adds seedSymptomJobRunLinkage(): applies label definitions to failed job runs, populates job_symptoms, and creates triage_symptoms.
  • Adds seedTestOutputsForPeriodicJobs(): seeds prow_job_run_test_outputs for 4 failed periodic-job test cases.
  • Adds 2 new component-readiness views (4.21-cross-compare, 4.22-flake-as-failure) to config/seed-views.yaml.
  • Adds e2e tests (test/e2e/release_payloads_test.go, test/e2e/test_outputs_test.go) exercising the new data through existing API endpoints.

Findings

No blocking, should-fix, or nit findings. This branch already carries a fixup commit (f3b0903d2) that addressed prior CodeRabbit feedback: previous-tag-linkage bug, non-deterministic Limit() queries, map-based label/symptom assignment races, and a multi-view regression-closing bug.
