Verdict

Approve

The removal is clean and complete — every reference to the dropped table, model, loader, and helper functions was found and removed, the down-migration faithfully restores the original schema, and the baseline-detection fallback was correctly repointed to a table that will survive this change. The only open items are process/ops questions (WIP/hold labels, a stale planning doc, and prod migration lock duration), not code defects.

What this PR does

  • Removes the test_analysis_by_job_by_dates GORM model, its BigQuery loader (loadDailyTestAnalysisByJob) and supporting types/functions, and the ProwLoaderTestAnalysis BQ label.
  • Removes partition registration for the table and its entries in the e2e partition lifecycle test.
  • Adds a migration to drop the dependent view prow_test_analysis_by_variant_14d_view, the table itself, and any detached partitions left behind by the partition lifecycle.
  • Removes now-dead helpers LoadProwJobCache and LoadTestCache (only callers were in the removed loader).
  • Repoints the baseline-migration existence check from the removed table to prow_job_run_tests, and rewrites a stale comment about plan_cache_mode=force_custom_plan.
